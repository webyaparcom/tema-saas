Data security and KVKK

When Akışpano processes a customer’s project data it acts as the data processor; the customer is the data controller. Processing terms are set out in a Data Processing Agreement.

Cross-border transfer (KVKK art. 9): On this demo site no data is sent to a server. In a real service data is stored in a domestic data centre; if a transfer abroad is needed, explicit consent or the safeguards listed in the law, together with the list of destination countries, are announced separately.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of the chosen term; a notice is sent by e-mail at least 7 days before renewal and you can cancel from account settings.
